Yunheng Zou

PhD Student in Computer Science, University of Toronto
Matter Lab · Vector Institute · Research Intern, NVIDIA Research

I am pursuing AI scientists: agents that can carry out deep, domain-heavy research and make scientific discovery faster and cheaper. I am interested in tackling hard problems in physics, chemistry and biology with a high degree of autonomy, by pairing domain-specialized agents with autonomous laboratories. I also work on the foundations this needs: benchmarks, data synthesis, and specialized RL environments for deep-domain tasks and domain adaptation. I lead El Agente, a family of autonomous agents for computational chemistry, advised by Prof. Alán Aspuru-Guzik and Prof. Varinia Bernales.

Research

My long-term goal is AI scientists that make research more scalable and cost-effective. I am especially interested in agents that tackle deep-science problems requiring real domain expertise, and in methods that let those agents adapt to a new scientific field on their own.

Training agents to do science

Multi-turn RL infrastructure and post-training recipes for agents: synthesizing verifiable RL tasks at scale, computer-use agents, SFT + RLVR for molecular manipulation, and chemistry verifiers such as reaction atom mapping.
